About Sutriva

Our Mission & Vision

Sutriva was founded with a clear mission: to eliminate the friction and fragmented chaos of travel planning. Travelers shouldn't have to bounce between four different booking apps just to compare flights, check train availability, find clean stays, and explore local food spots. Sutriva brings it all together in one harmonious, transparent platform with zero hidden markups.

Why Explorers Choose Sutriva

  • Unified Fare Matrix: Compare live flight, rail (IRCTC), and intercity bus routes side-by-side.
  • Live MongoDB Food Directory: Real-time discovery of 635+ local eateries, authentic messes, and dhabas with real verified price tags.
  • AI-Powered Travel Blueprint: Customized day-by-day itineraries generated around your exact pace, group, and budget.
